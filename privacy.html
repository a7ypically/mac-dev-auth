Mac Dev Backup privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026.

Mac Dev Backup is a personal utility running on its owner's Mac. It uses rclone and the Google Drive API to upload, list, verify, restore, and remove backup files as directed by its owner.

Google account access

The app requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. Access is limited to files created by the app or explicitly made accessible to it. It does not request access to Gmail, contacts, calendars, or unrelated Drive files.

Storage and use

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's Mac. Backup files and their associated metadata are stored in the Google Drive account authorized by the owner. Google API data is used only to perform the requested backup and restore operations.

The utility has no independently hosted application server. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties other than Google Drive as the selected storage provider. This public information site contains no backup data or credentials and uses no analytics.

Retention and revocation

The owner controls backup retention and can remove backup files from Google Drive. Access can be revoked in the Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not itself delete existing backups.

Contact

The app's support email is shown on its Google authorization screen.
